CunJi is a local-first native macOS disk-health utility from SHIXIN LAB. It reads SMART / NVMe health data from internal and local external disks, stores local snapshots, presents trends, and runs ordinary-file sequential read/write tests only after explicit user confirmation.

2. Privacy and Safety

- Health reads use only fixed, allowlisted, read-only smartctl arguments.
- The app never formats, repairs, mounts, unmounts, erases, or writes any raw disk device.
- Speed tests create ordinary temporary files and clean them up after completion or cancellation.
- Disk information is not uploaded in the background. Device identifiers such as serial numbers are hidden by default.
- External SMART availability depends on the disk, bridge chipset, and macOS pass-through support.
