## Requirements for Forks and Modified Builds / 分支与修改版要求

Forks and modified builds remain fully permitted under the GPL. Before public
distribution, they must:

1. use a clearly different product name and app icon;
2. remove statements that identify the build as an official SHIXIN LAB release;
3. preserve GPL, copyright, third-party, and corresponding-source notices;
4. state clearly that the project is an independent fork based on CunJi and is
   not endorsed by SHIXIN LAB;
5. avoid the official Bundle ID unless SHIXIN LAB has granted written
   permission.

GPL 允许创建分支和修改版。对外发布前，相关版本必须：

1. 使用明显不同的产品名称和 App 图标；
2. 删除把该版本描述为 SHIXIN LAB 官方发布的内容；
3. 保留 GPL、版权、第三方组件及对应源码声明；
4. 明确说明这是基于「存迹」的独立分支，未获得 SHIXIN LAB 官方背书；
5. 未经书面许可，不使用官方 Bundle ID。
