# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our Commitment / 我们的承诺

SHIXIN LAB welcomes respectful technical discussion and good-faith
contributions from people of every background and experience level. Project
spaces should remain safe, constructive, and focused on improving the software.

SHIXIN LAB 欢迎不同背景和经验水平的参与者进行尊重彼此的技术讨论与善意贡献。
项目空间应保持安全、建设性，并以改进软件为共同目标。

## Expected Conduct / 期望行为

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Explain safety, privacy, compatibility, and licensing consequences.
- Protect private device information and redact logs before sharing.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to preserve the documented product and
  safety boundaries.

- 尊重他人，反馈应具体且具有建设性。
- 讨论观点与代码，不攻击个人。
- 主动说明修改对安全、隐私、兼容性与许可证的影响。
- 分享日志前必须隐藏设备隐私信息。
- 尊重维护者为保护既定产品边界与安全边界所作的决定。

## Unacceptable Conduct / 不可接受的行为

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, or sustained
  disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Posting real disk serial numbers, UUIDs, UDIDs, private paths, credentials,
  or unredacted exports.
- Impersonating SHIXIN LAB or presenting an unofficial build as an official
  release.
- Intentionally weakening the read-only, privacy, licensing, or release
  integrity boundaries.

- 骚扰、威胁、歧视、人身攻击或持续扰乱讨论。
- 未经允许公开他人的私人信息。
- 发布真实硬盘序列号、UUID、UDID、私人路径、凭据或未脱敏导出。
- 冒充 SHIXIN LAB，或把非官方修改版描述为官方发布。
- 故意削弱只读、隐私、许可证或发布完整性边界。

## Reporting and Enforcement / 报告与执行

Do not place sensitive evidence in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities
must use the repository's private vulnerability reporting flow described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Other conduct concerns may be reported privately
through the contact route on the [SHIXIN LAB website](https://shixinqvq.com/).

请勿在公开 Issue 中提交敏感证据。安全漏洞必须通过
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 所述的 GitHub 私密漏洞报告渠道提交；其他行为问题
可通过 [SHIXIN LAB 官网](https://shixinqvq.com/)的联系渠道私下报告。

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions will be
based on the conduct, its impact, and the need to protect users and the project.

维护者可在必要时编辑或移除内容、关闭或锁定讨论、拒绝贡献或限制参与。处理将
依据具体行为、造成的影响，以及保护用户和项目的需要作出。

## Scope / 适用范围

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, release feedback, and
other public spaces where a participant represents this project.

本准则适用于仓库 Issue、Pull Request、Release 反馈，以及参与者代表本项目发言
的其他公开空间。
